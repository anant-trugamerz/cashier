#CUSTOMER HISTORY
The Cashier Program automatically maintains a history of every completed billing transaction for future reference.

--Billing Details--
After each billing cycle is completed, the following information is automatically saved to the software's database:
- Date and Time of Billing
- Cashier Name
- Customer Name
- Customer Mobile Number
- Items billed, along with their respective quantities and prices
- Net Total Amount Payable

--Storage Format--
Customer history is stored in a nested .json file format, allowing billing records to be efficiently organised and retrieved by the Cashier Program.

--Accessing Customer History--
Saved customer records can be viewed by interacting with the Cashier Program.
For information on the available customer history operations, refer to record_actions

--Notes--
- Billing records are saved automatically upon successful completion of a bill. No manual action is required.
- Customer records can be searched using the available record actions.
- Previous billing records cannot be modified through the Customer History module.
- Billing records are stored in chronological order based on the date and time of billing.
